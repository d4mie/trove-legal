Delete your Trove account

You can delete your Trove account in the app. You do not need to email anyone first.

  1. Open Trove and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Open Account Settings.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm.

That removes your account and the data tied to it from Trove’s backend, including your profile, saved recipes, collections, and taste preferences. Deleting the app from the phone removes the copy cached on that phone.

If you cannot open the app, use the contact email on the Trove Play listing and ask for the account to be deleted. Include the email address on the account.
