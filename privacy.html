Trove privacy policy

Last updated October 15, 2026.

Trove helps you find recipes, plan meals, and shop the ingredients.

The short version

Trove has an account so your recipes, collections, and taste preferences can sync and power social and discovery features. We show no ads, run no third-party analytics, do not sell your data, and do not track you across apps. Account data lives on Trove’s backend. Some state, such as your meal plan and grocery list, is also kept on your phone.

Your account

You sign in with email and password, or with Google. Trove’s backend stores your profile, the recipes and collections you save, your taste preferences, and the recipes you post. Posts you mark public, and your public profile, are visible to other people in Trove. Private posts are not.

What stays on your phone

A saved login stays in encrypted storage on the phone. Meal plans, grocery checks, and recent recipe views are cached on the phone. When you import a cooking video, the app listens to the clip and reads words on the frames on the phone. That audio is not sent to a speech service.

What leaves your phone

Recipe searches go to the recipe databases we use, as search words, not your identity. Your account and saved data go to Trove’s backend. A pasted Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube link is sent to Trove’s video service, with your sign-in, so the app can download that video. Shop links open Amazon in the browser.

If you connect a grocery store, Trove can use your approximate location, or a place you type, to find a store near you. That location, the product searches, and the items added to the cart go to that store under its own privacy policy. Trove does not share your location with other people.

Sign in with Google

Google sign-in is handled by Google under Google’s terms. Trove receives what it needs to create and identify your account.

What Trove does not do

No selling of data, no tracking across apps, no third-party analytics, and no ads.

Your control

You can delete your account in the app. The steps are on the account deletion page. Deleting the app removes the copy cached on your phone.
